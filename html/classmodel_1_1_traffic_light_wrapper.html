◆ getPhaseAnalysis()

int[] model.TrafficLightWrapper.getPhaseAnalysis ( )

Analyzes traffic to distinguish between stuck cars on red vs. moving cars on green. If a green lane is mostly jammed, we treat it as empty to force a switch and stop feeding the jam.

Returns
int array: [0] = waiting on RED, [1] = flow on GREEN
